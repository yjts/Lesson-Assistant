## Timed task

Start timing when the teacher first sees the builder. Stop when they say the lesson direction is useful enough to revise or use.

- Time to first useful lesson direction:
- Did the teacher complete generate, edit, save, reload, and print without assistance? Yes / No
- If assistance was needed, where?

## Ratings

Rate each item from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree).

- The inputs were clear.
- The inquiry question was useful.
- The six-stage structure was useful.
- Teacher and student roles were practical.
- Editing the generated direction was easy.
- Saving and finding a draft was easy.
- The printable summary was classroom-ready.
- I understood that standards and sources require my review.
- I would use this tool for another lesson.

## Open responses

- What was most useful?
- What was confusing or slowed you down?
- What important planning information was missing?
- What wording or curriculum content should change?
- Did anything make you worry about losing work or privacy?
- What is the single most important improvement before a wider pilot?

Do not include student names or student records in this feedback.
